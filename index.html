Privacy Policy

At Postalpro, your privacy is our top priority. We are committed to providing a secure and completely free educational environment for India Post employees.

1. Zero Data Tracking

We do not collect, track, or store any personal data, exam scores, or metadata from our users. Your visits remain completely anonymous.

2. Local Data Processing

Our utility tools, such as the TD Commission Generator, perform all mathematical calculations locally within your web browser. No financial or account data is ever sent to our servers. The PDF generation happens directly on your device, ensuring maximum security.

3. No Advertisements or Cookies

To ensure absolute ease of readability and zero distractions while you study or work, we do not employ third-party advertising trackers or invasive cookies.
