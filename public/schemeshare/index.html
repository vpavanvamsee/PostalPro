Postal Digital Security & Safe Attachment Handling Essential Rules

Simple, non-technical safety guidelines for Branch Postmasters & field postal staff

Never Open .APK, .EXE, or Unknown Installers

Fraudulent actors frequently disguise malware as "Department Updates" or "Postal Incentive Apps". India Post or PostalPro will never send you .apk files on WhatsApp.

Verify Attachment Senders First

Before clicking any PDF, invoice, or image received via email or WhatsApp, check the sender's full email or phone number. When in doubt, confirm through a direct phone call.

Never Disclose OTPs or CSI/SAP Passwords

No departmental officer, inspector, or admin will ever ask for your portal login passwords, Darpan MPIN, or phone OTPs. Keep them strictly confidential.

Official Postal Communication Channels

Official SchemeShare administrator communications originate exclusively from postalpro.in@gmail.com or verified WhatsApp group contacts. Report any suspicious links.
