Frequently Asked Questions

How is the TD Commission calculated for India Post BPMs?
Commission is calculated based on the principal deposit amount: 0.5% for 1-Yr, 1.0% for 2-Yr, 1.5% for 3-Yr, and 2.0% for 5-Yr accounts. Our tool calculates this automatically.
Where should I submit the printed TD Bill?
The printed Form-1 schedule is signed by the BPM and submitted to your Account Office / Sub-Post Office (SO) along with the monthly accounts for sanction.
Can I save my Branch details for next month?
Yes! PostalPro automatically saves your BO Name, SO Name, Pincode, and Division in your browser's encrypted vault so you never have to re-enter them.
