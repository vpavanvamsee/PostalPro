Frequently Asked Questions

What are the current bonus rates for PLI policies?
For Whole Life Assurance (Suraksha), bonus is ₹76 per ₹1,000 sum assured per year. For Endowment Assurance (Santosh), bonus is ₹52 per ₹1,000 sum assured per year.
Who is eligible for Rural Postal Life Insurance (RPLI)?
All residents living in rural areas of India are eligible for RPLI plans like Gram Santosh, Gram Suraksha, and Gram Suvidha.
Is customer information safe in this tool?
Yes. PostalPro stores all lead details locally inside your device using browser encrypted storage. No customer information is ever transmitted to any external server.
